Reading these figures

Comparison parcels are mostly near their LIHTC, so large zones overlap heavily. Similar shares at 5 km are expected from that overlap and do not show that the surroundings are alike.

Land use comes from a different source in each city. In Milwaukee, a vacant lot is a taxable parcel with no building value, and exempt vacant lots count as Exempt, so Vacant is likely understated there. Compare differences within a city more readily than levels between cities.

Many apartments lie outside the LIHTC's own tract, so the apartment comparison is less local than the residential one. The intervals are not adjusted for the number of comparisons shown.
